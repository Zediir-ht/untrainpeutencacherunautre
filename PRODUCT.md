## Users
Priority order confirmed by the user: (1) artists and local public (people who might join, attend or support), (2) artists (applicants to calls for projects, future members), (3) visitors and patrons (come to exhibitions, donate).

## Product Purpose
Website of the association "Un train peut en cacher un autre" (Cazals, Lot, Bouriane / Périgord Noir border). It promotes, supports and encourages contemporary art ("art actuel") in rural areas, gets artists out of isolation, and organizes collective exhibitions in villages. Success: more members, more attendance, more donations, more applicants to calls for projects.

## Positioning
Artist-run association founded in 2009 that brings contemporary art to villages and unusual places (a house, a church, a railway-station-turned-gallery, land-art trails), free and open to the public. The name is a railway pun and the exhibitions have a history of train/station imagery (Marminiac Gart, Errances).

## Operating Context
Annual membership year runs November to October. Collective exhibitions each year in rural venues. Calls for projects (appel à projet). Tax deductions: up to 66% on donations, leave-day donation (law of 2024), deductions on art purchases. Practical sections of the current site: Accueil, Actualité, Adhésions, Les artistes, Photos/vidéos, Cours et stages, Livre d'or, Contact, Boutique, Historique, Liens.

## Product Principles
1. Keep real information findable under the joke: join, give, visit, contact.
2. The train pun is the engine: one thing always hides behind another.
3. Rural and artist-run, not institutional; humor is warm, never sarcastic toward artists or villages.
4. Never fabricate facts, dates or artworks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Humor must not block access: respect prefers-reduced-motion, keyboard navigation and readable contrast; jokes never replace labels on core actions.
